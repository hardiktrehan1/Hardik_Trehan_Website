About Me

Risk professional.
Statistical thinker.
Market strategist.

I'm Hardik Trehan — an FRM Level 2 Candidate (Level 1 cleared, top quartile) and a practitioner of investment risk analytics, statistics, and market strategy. I am currently pursuing a dual-degree Master's programme in Business Statistics and Finance, alongside Columbia University's Financial Engineering & Risk Management Specialization.

I hold dual bachelor's degrees — a BBA in Banking, Financial Services & Insurance (Minor: Risk Management, Valedictorian, CGPA 9.53/10, Grade O Outstanding) and a B.Com in Finance & Accounting (First Division). I am also certified as CMSA®, FPWMP®, and FTIP® through the Corporate Finance Institute.

Previously, I served in a Financial Risk Advisory role at Grant Thornton Bharat LLP, delivering enterprise risk assessments, internal control reviews, and regulatory compliance projects across fintechs, banks, and NBFCs — covering GRC, control testing, operational risk, regulatory compliance, and market risk. I was awarded the SPOT Award for outstanding project delivery.

My analytical foundation spans VaR/CVaR, stress testing, GARCH volatility modelling, yield curve analysis, derivatives pricing (Black–Scholes, Monte Carlo, Vasicek/CIR/Hull-White), stochastic calculus, and machine learning applied to financial econometrics. I bring additional experience from equity research at SMC Global Securities, offering a holistic view of capital markets and investment risk.
